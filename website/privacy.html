Overview

RSS Cloud Sync is developed by Razeen Secure Solution. The app is designed to synchronize files between a device and supported cloud storage services.

Data and permissions

The app may require access to folders selected by the user and authorization for connected cloud providers. Access is used to perform the synchronization actions requested by the user.

Third-party services

When a user connects a cloud provider, that provider's own privacy policy and terms also apply to its service. RSS Cloud Sync does not claim ownership of files selected for synchronization.
